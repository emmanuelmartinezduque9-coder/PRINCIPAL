# PROMPT OFICIAL DE ANÁLISIS DE VÍDEO (Emmanuel) — usar SIEMPRE en el pipeline

> El análisis de Higgsfield da escenas y tiempos, pero es POBRE para replicar.
> Este prompt de 10 puntos es el que produce los prompts buenos (de aquí salió RECETA COCINA MMS).
> Claude produce este desglose combinando: escenas+tiempos de Higgsfield + los fotogramas extraídos del vídeo.
>
> ⚠️ ADAPTACIÓN OBLIGATORIA AL USARLO PARA GENERAR:
> - NO incluir captions/textos superpuestos de la competencia en el vídeo a generar.
> - El producto es SIEMPRE el de la cuenta (y se pasa su imagen como referencia).
> - Idioma/mercado según la cuenta.

You are analyzing a dropshipping product video. This is a product-centered video. Analyze it across these 10 points and give me a detailed breakdown:

HOOK
How does the video open in the first 1-3 seconds?
What visual element grabs attention immediately?
What is the hook technique used (curiosity, contrast, movement, product reveal)?

SCRIPT STRUCTURE
What is the overall narrative flow of the video?
How is the product introduced, demonstrated, and closed?
What is the total estimated duration?

SCENE BY SCENE — For each scene describe:
What the hands are doing
Camera angle (top-down, side, close-up, macro)
Surface/table type and color
Lighting style (soft, hard, natural, ring light, side light)
Background (solid color, textured, neutral, dark, white)
Estimated duration of the scene

PRODUCT ZONES
Which parts of the product are shown and in what order?
How many times is the product fully visible vs. partially shown?
Are there macro/detail shots? Which product features do they highlight?

PACING & CUTS
How fast or slow are the cuts?
Is there a rhythm pattern (fast-fast-slow or consistent)?
Are there any transitions? What type?
How many total cuts in the video?

PRODUCT PRESENTATION
How is the product physically handled?
Is it unboxed, assembled, used, or simply displayed?
What angles best show the product value?

HAND TECHNIQUE
How are the hands positioned (grip style, open palm, pinch)?
Is there rotation of the product? How many times and in which direction?
What gestures are used to highlight product features?
Are nails visible? What do they look like (natural, manicured, gloves)?

CTA (Call to Action)
Is there a CTA? If yes, how is it delivered (visual, gesture, text)?
At what point in the video does the CTA appear?
What action is the viewer being driven toward?

VIRAL FORMULA
What is the core emotional or psychological trigger of this video?
Why would someone stop scrolling for this?
What makes it feel authentic and not like an ad?

REPLICATION GUIDE — Give me a step-by-step guide to replicate this video from scratch:
Setup (surface, background, lighting)
Hand movements sequence
Scene order and timing
Camera angles to use
Product handling technique
Pacing and cut rhythm

FACE / AVATAR ANALYSIS — If a face, person, character, mascot, or avatar appears, analyze type, first appearance and duration, relationship with the product, expression, gaze direction, how much is visible, visual traits for replication (age category, style, hair, makeup, accessories, clothing colors), its selling role, whether it reads as UGC or ad, number of appearances and a per-appearance breakdown (timestamp, framing, expression, product interaction, background, duration), and whether the video could be replicated without it. Do not identify a real person or infer sensitive personal traits.
If none: state exactly "No face or avatar is visible in this video." and do not invent one.

FACE / AVATAR REPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Specify whether a real person, model, hand-only creator, mascot, toy, illustrated figure, or animated/AI avatar is needed; the exact moments to recreate; recommended framing, expression, head movement, eye direction, product interaction, lighting and duration for each appearance. Keep it product-centered.

AUDIO RULES FOR REPLICATION
Use ASMR ambient sound only (natural sounds of the product, hands, surface)
No background music
No voiceover or dialogue
No text overlays, captions, or on-screen graphics of any kind
